# Level 20 - MPLS

Covers Multiprotocol Label Switching, the foundation of modern service-provider and enterprise WAN networks. Explains MPLS basics and label-switched forwarding, the roles of label edge and label switching routers, the LFIB and FEC concepts, and the use of VRF and MP-BGP to deliver MPLS VPN services.
