🧪 Labs — consolidating the whole CCNP path

The final level ties the entire ENCOR curriculum together through hands-on lab scenarios: choose a platform, then build design labs (campus/branch/ISP/DC), drill protocol labs, and finish with automation + end-to-end integration.

Design

Strategy: use a free/low-fidelity tool (Packet Tracer) for ENCOR basics + CLI practice, then a real-image platform (CML/GNS3/EVE-NG) for the deep lab scenarios.

🗺️ The Full Path Recap

This capstone closes the 30-level CCNP Enterprise path. The lab strategy ties every earlier level together:

Foundations

L1-L4, addressing, VLAN/STP, routing

Routing & services

OSPF/EIGRP/BGP, redistribution, FHRP, NAT, QoS

WAN & security

MPLS, DMVPN/IPsec, SD-WAN, VPN, wireless

Automation & operations

SDN/YANG, virtualization/cloud, monitoring, HA, IOS-XE, troubleshooting

Practice in a real-image lab (CML/GNS3/EVE-NG), build each design from the ground up, drill the protocol labs, then automate and verify end-to-end. Hands-on + reproducibility = exam confidence.
