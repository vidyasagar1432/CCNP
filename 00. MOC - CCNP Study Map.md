# CCNP Enterprise (ENCOR 350-401) — Study Map

> A guided path through the 31 level folders of this vault, from foundational networking to enterprise automation, with pointers to the right levels, priorities, and cross-cutting themes.

## How to Use This Vault
- Study each level in the recommended order below: read the level's notes and `index.md` first to get the conceptual framework.
- After reading, review each topic's flashcards via `index.html` (double-click to open; it loads `decks.js` built from the per-topic JSON decks).
- Use the SRS app to schedule spaced repetition — treat the flashcard decks as your active-recall engine, not a substitute for reading the notes.
- Rebuild decks after adding/editing JSON with `python3 build-decks.py`, then refresh `index.html`.
- Use the Exam Weights and Cross-Cutting Themes sections below to prioritize weak areas and reinforce connections between levels.

## Cross-Cutting Themes

- **Spanning Tree** runs through [[Level 08 - STP/index]] (the protocol itself), [[Level 29 - Troubleshooting/index]] (loop diagnosis), and [[Level 30 - Labs/index]] (config/verification practice).
- **First Hop Redundancy** in [[Level 14 - First Hop Redundancy/index]] depends on [[Level 07 - VLAN Technologies/index]] and [[Level 05 - IPv4/index]] for gateway addressing, and pairs with [[Level 27 - High Availability/index]] for gateway failover.
- **Route Redistribution** in [[Level 13 - Route Redistribution/index]] links OSPF ([[Level 10 - OSPF/index]]), EIGRP ([[Level 11 - EIGRP/index]]), and BGP ([[Level 12 - BGP/index]]) together — a common theme in [[Level 29 - Troubleshooting/index]].
- **NAT/PAT** in [[Level 15 - NAT/index]] builds on IPv4 fundamentals ([[Level 05 - IPv4/index]]) and recurs in [[Level 17 - Security/index]] and [[Level 23 - Enterprise WAN/index]] designs.
- **VPNs** in [[Level 18 - VPN Technologies/index]] relate to the security and encrypting protocols of [[Level 17 - Security/index]] and to WAN connectivity in [[Level 23 - Enterprise WAN/index]].
- **QoS** in [[Level 21 - QoS/index]] ties into [[Level 22 - Wireless/index]] (WLAN QoS), [[Level 20 - MPLS/index]] (MPLS-based QoS), and voice/video across [[Level 23 - Enterprise WAN/index]].
- **Automation & telemetry** in [[Level 24 - SDN & Automation/index]] and [[Level 26 - Monitoring & Telemetry/index]] rely on [[Level 28 - Cisco IOS and IOS XE/index]] for device APIs (NETCONF/RESTCONF) and on [[Level 25 - Virtualization & Cloud/index]] for programmatic environments.
- **Troubleshooting & labs** in [[Level 29 - Troubleshooting/index]] and [[Level 30 - Labs/index]] synthesize virtually every other level, making them the practical capstone of the study path.
