# MAN

## Definition

A **Metropolitan Area Network (MAN)** spans the scale of a **city or metropolitan region** — bigger than a campus [[LAN]] in scope but smaller than a **[[WAN]]**. It typically connects multiple sites of one organization (or a provider's metro customers) across a metro area.

## Where It Fits

```text
PAN (personal)  <  LAN (building/campus)  <  MAN (city)  <  WAN (wide)
```

- Real deployments: campus-to-campus connectivity in one city, metro Ethernet services, DWDM/CWDM rings between data centers.
- Often delivered as **Metro Ethernet** or dark fiber by a provider, but with metro-grade latency.

## Exam Focus

- **The scale ladder is the usual question:** place each type on the geographic spectrum correctly.
- MAN frequently appears as "the city-wide link between two of your buildings" — a WAN would be overkill, a LAN too small.
- Know that metro Ethernet is a typical MAN technology.
