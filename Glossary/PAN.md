# PAN

## Definition

A **Personal Area Network (PAN)** covers the smallest scope: the **space around a single person** — connecting a phone, laptop, watch, headphones, and peripherals over short-range radio or cable.

## Examples

- Bluetooth/Wi-Fi Direct between phone and earbuds or keyboard.
- USB tethering, wireless mouse/keyboard.
- Near-field device pairing (< 10 m links).

## Exam Focus

- **PAN is the smallest rung** of the classification ladder (PAN < LAN < MAN < WAN) — the exam asks placement, not details.
- "Short-range radio technology" questions map to Bluetooth/IR solutions, i.e., PAN scope.
- Contrast: PAN is personal; LAN is organizational — a common trap.
